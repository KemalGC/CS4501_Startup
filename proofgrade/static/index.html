What notation can I use?

Use one variable, x, and linear or quadratic equations. Multiplication can be written as 2x or 2*x. Fractions use /; division by a variable is outside this version’s scope.

Write square roots as sqrt(2). Put both quadratic roots on the same line: x=-sqrt(2) or x=sqrt(2). You can also enter no real solutions or all real numbers.

Up to 20 steps and 256 characters per step. Unsupported notation will be flagged for revision or manual review.
