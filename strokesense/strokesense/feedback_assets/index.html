This study uses eights. Judge only the seats and movements you can see; hidden seats do not prove eight correct detections. Use Partly correct for a mix of supported and unsupported details, Cannot judge when the view is insufficient, and No applicable output when there is no relevant claim.

Body angles are projections in the camera view. A hidden joint or a different camera angle cannot establish an exact physical angle. Your judgments remain human feedback, separate from a measured benchmark.

Follow one clearly visible rower. Count consecutive catches including the first and last, then record their times. Five catches contain four complete stroke intervals. Use the same rower and time window as the model claim when comparing.

Rate = 60 × (catches − 1) ÷ elapsed seconds. This is your manual observation, not verified ground truth; it does not automatically score the model.
